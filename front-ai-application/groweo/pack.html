Content audit

Strong product pages. Very little that answers buyers' questions.

We reviewed the home page, the models page, the move-in-ready page and the quote form. The site sells well to someone who has already decided. It does little for someone who is still searching.

Home page: the newest content is the NopsaKoti switch, a Ruka project and a customer-satisfaction post.

Footer: 2 latest articles, both company news, and 1 customer story.

What's there

  • Clear model cards with prices and sizes (11 models)
  • A good explainer page: "Muuttovalmis talopaketti"
  • A few older guide-style posts: ASP loans, "Mitä muita kuluja…", interior design
  • An FAQ page linked in the top bar
  • The Groweo quiz, chat and quote form

What's missing

  • Pages built around the questions buyers type into Google or ChatGPT
  • An explanation of "NopsaKoti" in the words buyers use: modular, prefab, move-in-ready
  • A regular flow of content. The latest posts are news
  • Links from content to the quiz (the quiz is only on the home and models pages)

Scorecard (our view, from 4 pages)

Product information Lead capture (Groweo) Price transparency Answers to buyer questions Fresh content Ready for AI answers (GEO)

This is a qualitative view, not a measured SEO score. The AI Reach analysis would replace it with real data.
