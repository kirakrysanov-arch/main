Today: 2,426 listings, no chat, and the English pages answer in Finnish

Bo promotes "English-speaking professionals", homes in Lapland and an office in Marbella. On the English site, though, the listing details, the description and the costs are all in Finnish, and every question goes to a single agent's phone, email or form.

bo.fi/en, in Finnish

English page, Finnish content

On bo.fi/en, listing Parantolankatu 7–9 has its description, "Huoneisto", "Asumiskustannukset" and "Hinta" all in Finnish. An international buyer can't read the costs.

2

Every question goes to one person

The options are a phone number, an email, the agent's calendar and a 4-field form. "What's the maintenance charge?" at 21:40 waits until the next morning.
