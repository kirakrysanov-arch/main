The problem

You invested in automation.
Your customers still call.

Phone and e-mail are habits. Without a nudge, customers go to the channel they know, even when an app or a bot could solve it in seconds.

⏳

Queues cost twice

Every avoidable call costs agent time and customer patience. The callers who really need a human wait longer too.

🤖

Self-service sits unused

Your chatbot, app and forms are good at their jobs. They just aren't where customers look first.

🔍

No one sees the full picture

Channel data lives in five tools. Nobody can say which topics should go where, or whether they actually do.

6
How reChanneld works

Map. Nudge. Learn.

A no-code console your customer service team owns. Your IT team doesn't need to build anything.

1

Map your channel strategy

Decide topic by topic where each request should go: app, bot, form, chat or phone. Set different rules by time of day or queue length.

💳 Card blocking→ App
🏠 Mortgage questions→ Chat → Expert
⚠️ Fraud→ Phone, priority
2

Nudge, don't block

Customers see the best option first, with honest wait times and smart defaults. Every channel stays open. The easy path just becomes the obvious one.
