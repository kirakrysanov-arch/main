Your website, exactly as it is.

Scene 1 · Homepage
A subscriber arrives. They now have somewhere to go.
Today the homepage serves advertisers, investors and job seekers, and nothing on it is for subscribers. reChanneld adds one small “Subscriber help” button that opens the widget. It can also be embedded directly on a help or contact page. Nothing else on the page moves.

Scene 2 · The reChanneld widget opens
Topic, then the best channel, with the most popular choice highlighted.
The subscriber picks a topic (Delivery) and then their exact need (Paper didn't arrive). reChanneld shows the channels side by side, highlights the most popular choice, and is upfront about opening hours and waiting times. This is reChanneld's standard widget with DC Thomson's own topics.

Scene 3 · The footer
“Cancellation Form” stays where it is. Now it opens the right options.
Point the existing link at the widget's “Change or cancel” topic. Subscribers see that they can pause or switch to digital, and cancelling online is offered as an equal choice, never hidden. The Contact box can open the widget the same way, so subscribers, advertisers and press each find the right place.
