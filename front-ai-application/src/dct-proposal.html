How it works

  • One front door on each title's help page, app and e-mails. Readers pick their topic instead of guessing a number.
  • The fastest route goes first: self-service, help article, form or the right team, with honest opening hours and wait times.
  • Every channel stays open. We guide; we never block.
  • Rules set by your CX team in a simple console, by title, topic, time of day and queue length.
  • Live insight into which topics reach which channel, title by title.

A thought on cancellations

Cancelling should stay as easy as subscribing, and UK subscription rules point the same way. A front door can still offer the reader a pause, a switch to digital, or a quick chat next to the cancel option. Many readers who “cancel” only wanted a break.

Nothing to replace

It sits on top of your existing help centre, My Account and subscription management with Air Business. No new chatbot, CRM or IT project.
