2. Rules for all content

  • Lead with the customer's problem and a number. Product features go last.
  • One proof point per piece, with the customer named and the source stated.
  • Write in the founders' own words. I draft, they edit, and nothing goes out in a voice they wouldn't use.
  • Finnish for Finnish public sector and SMEs, English for UK and international accounts and for most LinkedIn posts.
  • Every piece ends in one action: book a demo, see the case or reply.

Post 1 · Mikko · week 3

One of our customers tested what their AI agent does without us.

They had reChanneld on their busiest pages, asking visitors what they need and sending each topic to the right place, including the AI agent.

With reChanneld, AI-agent conversations went up 44%.
They removed it for two months: −62%.
They put it back: +107%.

The agent was the same the whole time. What changed was whether people found it.

If your AI agent is quiet, look at what happens before people reach it.

Post 2 · Touko · week 3

A one-hour exercise for any customer service lead:

1. Export last month's contacts and list the 10 most common reasons.
2. For each, write the channel that answers it best: self-service, chat, AI agent or phone.
3. Open your help page and pretend to be that customer. Where does the page send you?

In most audits we do, the phone number is the most visible option for all 10.

That gap is your channel strategy.

9. Newsletter

Recommendation: yes, but small and from month 2. A newsletter with no list doesn't create pipeline in 90 days. It becomes useful once there are people to send it to: existing customers, webinar sign-ups, demo requests and accounts that replied to outbound. Start it in week 8, right after the webinar, when the first real list exists.

Two channels, same content

ChannelAudienceWhyTool
LinkedIn newsletter on Mikko's profileFollowers and connections (the 200 target contacts after outbound)No list needed. LinkedIn notifies subscribers, and it builds Mikko's profile with the people Touko is contactingLinkedIn (free)
EmailCustomers, webinar sign-ups, demo requests and opt-ins from the website formOwned list. Drives referrals and expansion with customers, and keeps warm leads warmHubSpot free marketing email (check the monthly send limit)

Email goes only to customers and people who opted in (form checkbox, webinar registration). Cold outbound contacts are not added to the newsletter list.

Format

  • Name: "Channel Notes" (working title). Monthly, first Tuesday. English, plus a Finnish email version for Finnish public sector and customers.
  • Length: readable in 3 minutes. Plain layout that looks like an email from Mikko, not a brochure.
  • Fixed structure: 1) one number from a customer and what caused it, 2) one practical tip for service leaders, 3) one thing we learned from the data (4.3M sessions a year), 4) one link: case, webinar recording or demo.
  • It reuses the month's LinkedIn posts and case material, so it adds about 2 hours of work per issue.
