Prepared by Kira (Knock) for Mikko and Touko · October 2026 · Draft for discussion. Numbers marked assumption are replaced with real data in week 1.

1. Starting point

  • No marketing owner, budget, plan, tracking or recurring activity. No social media and no paid campaigns.
  • The website rebrand is with the founder and has no date. The only sales material is one deck and a few case slides.
  • Pipeline comes from Touko's and the founders' networks. Nothing is measured, so we don't know the conversion rate at any stage.
  • What works in our favour: 7 customer cases with hard numbers and named people (Lippu.fi, S-Bank, CDON, Laurea, Nets/Nexi, Keusote, plus one AI-agent case), 20+ recognisable Finnish logos, and fast implementation (8 business days at Lippu.fi, 2 weeks at Nets).

Positioning, one line (founders to finalise)

reChanneld sits on your customer service pages and guides each customer to the right channel (self-service, AI agent or a person) before they contact you.

The AI-agent angle leads in 2026 because it attaches reChanneld to budgets companies already spent. Cost reduction is the second message, and CX the third.
