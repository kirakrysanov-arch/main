03 · Five findings from borjessonsbil.se

A good chat, with office hours

01

The chat is staffed weekdays 09:00–17:00 only Key finding

“Chatten … är tillgänglig alla vardagar 09:00–17:00.” That's 40 of the week's 168 hours. Evenings and weekends, when many people browse cars, have no answer.

Smilee answers the other 128 hours, in Swedish.
02

The chat closes an hour before customer service High

Customer service is open weekdays 09:00–18:00, the chat 09:00–17:00. The busy after-work hour has no chat.

Smilee covers 17:00–09:00 and weekends, with hand-over to the hosts in the morning.
03

Trade-in and financing require a person High

Step 4 of their buying guide: “Kontakta oss när du hittat rätt – vi hjälper dig med inbyte, finansiering och det praktiska.”

CarWebshop's trade-in valuation and financing calculator, inside the chat.
04

The contact form promises a reply within 24 hours Medium

“…kontakta oss så återkopplar vi till dig inom 24 timmar.” A buyer writing on Friday evening may hear back on Monday, and may have bought elsewhere by then.

Instant answers, and a test drive booked in the same conversation.
05

Seven sites, one chat Medium

Buyers must pick the right site in forms (“Välj anläggning”). The chat example on the site ends in “boka ett möte med en säljare”.

Smilee routes each lead to the right site's sales team, with car, trade-in and slot attached.
