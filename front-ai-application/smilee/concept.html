No redesign. These are borjessonsbil.se pages as they are today, with Smilee CarWebshop added. It answers buyers in Swedish in the evening and at the weekend, values their trade-in, books the test drive and hands a warm lead to the right site in the morning.

Scene 1 · Today
A good chat, with office hours.
Börjessons already invests in chat: human “digital hosts” welcome visitors by name. But the chat is only open on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00, an hour shorter than customer service. The buying guide asks buyers to get in touch for trade-in and financing, and the contact form promises a reply “within 24 hours”.
