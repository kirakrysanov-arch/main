## Product (from smilee.ai screenshots, 6 Oct 2026)
- **AI Chatbot:** "Serve better. Sell more." A 24/7 AI agent on the website and WhatsApp, with CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier) and "Nordic expertise".
- **CarWebshop:** "turns your dealership site into an online store".
  - Automated lead capture, 24/7 service, test-drive booking, trade-in valuation, financing calculator, and a sales pipeline dashboard.
  - Claimed results: **+45% more leads, 24/7, 0s response time**.
  - "Get 10 free leads every month". Setup "less than a week" / "up and running in as little as 48 hours".
- **Demos** include "I want to buy this car: FI DEMO / **SWE DEMO**", so Sweden is clearly the next market.
- Positioning: "The digital partner for car dealerships **in Finland**".
- **Widget look:** navy chat bubbles, white text, rounded corners, "Powered by Smilee", bot name "CarBot", 👋 greeting.
