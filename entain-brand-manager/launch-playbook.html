Influencers, streamers, partners, sponsorshipsResponsibility 7

Works today
Streamer deals and creator codes.

Influencers out Sponsorship stays
Influencer marketing through third-party accounts ends. Sponsorships stay possible if they don't promote gambling directly and don't reach under-18s. I'd move the budget into adult sports properties and brand-owned content on our own channels, cleared with legal first.

Affiliate and acquisition channelsResponsibility 9

Works today
Commission-based comparison sites drive a large share of sign-ups.

Banned
Affiliate marketing aimed at Finnish players is prohibited. Acquisition has to come from brand: search demand, direct traffic and word of mouth. That shift is what the KPIs below track.

PlatformATL · Web

Nyt sinulla on valinta.

Valinta

"Now you have a choice."

For decades Finns had one option. The launch line takes the one thing that's actually new, choice, and links it to control: limits, time-outs and clear odds, shown up front.

  • TV and BVOD 20" spots built around a player setting their own limit
  • OOH in Helsinki, Tampere and Turku during launch week
  • "Luvallinen Suomessa" (licensed in Finland) as the trust mark everywhere
SponsorshipAdult sport

Kotikenttä.

Kotikenttä

"Home ground."

Partnerships with adult clubs in hockey and pesäpallo towns, using the brand name only, with no gambling message. They pay for local things fans notice, like rink lighting or away-day buses, and give the brand a local presence that affiliates never could.

  • Each property checked for under-18 audience share before signing
  • Brand name only, no odds or offers in the venue
  • Success measured by regional awareness change, not clicks
Owned contentWeb · Social

Selvää suomea.

Selvää suomea

"In plain Finnish."

A tone of voice that explains instead of hypes. What a 2.10 price means, what RTP is, how to set a limit in ten seconds. It replaces the education affiliates used to do and it builds trust while doing it.

  • Weekly match previews on our own site, written in-house
  • Non-interactive explainer series on verified social accounts
  • Swedish-language version (På ren svenska) for Finland's Swedish speakers
