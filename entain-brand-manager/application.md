## 1. What you need to know about the company

- **Entain NCE** (North & Central Europe) is part of Entain plc (Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin, partypoker, BetMGM JV). It has 850+ people, its HQ is in Riga, and it has offices in Tallinn, Vilnius, Amsterdam, Malta, Berlin, Vienna and Helsinki. It is the leading operator in the Baltics, and its flagship brands include **Optibet** (from the Enlabs acquisition). The company tagline is **"It's your game!"**
- **The reason this job exists now:** Finland's Gambling Act passed on 16 Dec 2025 (over 94% of MPs voted for it). Licence applications opened **1 March 2026**, Entain is a confirmed applicant, and the **market opens 1 July 2027**. Veikkaus keeps lotteries, scratch cards and land-based slots. Online casino and betting become licensed, with 5-year licences and **22% tax on GGR**. Around 40–50 licensees are expected.
- **The detail most applicants will miss:** under the new Act, **affiliate marketing and influencer marketing are banned** for operators targeting Finns. Social media is only allowed on the operator's own verified accounts, and only non-interactively. Sponsorship is OK as long as it doesn't promote gambling directly and doesn't target under-18s. All marketing must be "moderate". The job ad still lists affiliates and influencers (responsibilities 7 and 9) because they're legal until launch. A candidate who already plans for the switch stands out, and the creative piece is built around that.
- **Supervision** moves from the National Police Board to a new national supervisory authority on 1.7.2027. That's the "meetings with inspectors" part of the job.

## 5. Interview prep: questions they will likely ask

1. *How do you acquire players without affiliates?* Brand-created search demand, owned editorial content, adult sponsorship, and ATL that's consistent rather than loud. Measure it with branded search share and the direct/organic share of sign-ups.
2. *How do you work with compliance without killing creativity?* Get legal involved at the brief stage, not the final-asset stage. Keep a pre-cleared message bank and an approval log.
3. *Veikkaus has decades of trust. How do you compete?* Not head-on. Go after choice and control: transparency, limits, fair odds.
4. *Walk us through a budget you managed.* Bring numbers.

**Questions to ask them:** Which brand will this role own in Finland? How much of current FI acquisition is affiliate-driven? What is the brand-vs-performance budget split for 2027?
