FAQ

Good questions

How much of this is automated?

Research, drafting and layout run through semi-automated workflows, which is why a pack takes days instead of weeks. I choose the angle, check every fact against its source and edit every line. Anything I can't verify is marked as such.

Who sends the messages?

Your team, from their own LinkedIn and e-mail. The packs are written in your seller's voice and can be sent the same day.

Do you redesign our prospects' websites?

No. The concept shows the prospect's real pages exactly as they are, with only your product added. That's what makes it credible.

What do you need from us?

A 30-minute kick-off, your product's real screenshots or a demo, and the accounts you want. I'll ask before assuming anything about what your product can do.

Is our target list kept confidential?

Yes. Your accounts, packs and results are yours, never shown to anyone else, and never used in my own marketing without your written permission.

Which markets and languages?

Finland, the Nordics and the UK, in English or Finnish.
