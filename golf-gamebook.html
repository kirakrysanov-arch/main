TikTok / Reels script · series

"The Honest Scorecard"

A recurring short-form series about the gap between the score golfers claim at the bar and the score their scorecard shows. It's relatable, a little cheeky, and the app is the hero without a hard sell.

0:00–0:02 · Hook"He told the bar he shot 82."Clubhouse, pint raised, confident nod.
0:02–0:06 · Turn"His friends were keeping score."Phone screen: the live leaderboard updates.
0:06–0:10 · Reveal91. With a 9 on the par 3.Zoom on the scorecard. Friends lose it.
0:10–0:13 · Close"Every stroke counts. Literally."End card. Comment prompt: "What did you tell the bar?"
