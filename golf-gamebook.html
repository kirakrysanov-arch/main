Three ideas for GameBook

What I'd make this season

October · push & Instagram

The Last Round

Nordic courses are closing for winter. Nudge golfers to play one last round with friends, then share the community's frosty final scorecards.

Most Finnish courses close this month. Got one last round in you? 3 friends are playing Sunday.

December · push, email & creators

The Season Card

A shareable "your year in golf" recap built from rounds golfers already logged. Every share is an ad made by a golfer.

Your 2026 season, scored. One personal best, and a hole that owes you an apology.

Ongoing · TikTok & Reels

The Honest Scorecard

A short video series about the score golfers claim at the bar versus the one their friends recorded in the app.

"He told the bar he shot 82. His friends were keeping score."
